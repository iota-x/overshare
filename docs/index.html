The app itself

Everything above has a dial.

Eight pages, and the window checks its own work — it verifies your webhook as you type and lists the AI models your key can actually reach. Changes apply while it's running.

Getting it running

Two minutes, no terminal.

01

Install it

Drag it to Applications, or run the Windows installer. First launch needs right-click → Open on macOS, or More info → Run anyway on Windows — it isn't signed by a paid developer account.

02

Paste one link

In Discord, right-click a channel → Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → Copy Webhook URL. Paste it into Setup. Telegram works too, or both at once.

03

Nothing

It starts telling her straight away. The AI, the recaps, the blocklist — all optional, all already sensible.

Where it all goes

To her. And nowhere else.

There's no account, no server of ours in the middle, and nothing to sign up for. It talks to your chat app and stops there.

  • It stays on your machine. Settings and history live in one folder. Delete it and nothing of you remains.
  • The blocklist ships on. Password managers and private windows are hidden before you've configured anything.
  • Peeking is loud. She can't look at your camera or screen without you being told, and either can be switched off.
  • One click stops everything. Pause from the menu bar. She sees that updates stopped, not what you're doing.
Reasonable questions

Yes, it's a bit much.

Does she need to install anything?

No — just Discord or Telegram, which she almost certainly already has. Everything runs on your computer.

Is this healthy?

It started because someone said “you never tell me anything”, and it's meant for two people who both find it funny. There's a pause button and a blocklist for the moments you don't.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The app is free, Groq hands out free API keys, and Ollama runs on your own machine. Only Claude bills you, and it's optional.

Why does macOS say it can't verify it?

Because it isn't signed by a paid Apple developer account, which costs $99 a year. Right-click → Open once and macOS remembers.

Will it slow my computer down?

No. It reads a couple of window titles every few seconds, and you can make it check less often.

Read it before you run it This app watches your screen. You shouldn't take anyone's word for what it does with that — every line is on GitHub, MIT licensed.
