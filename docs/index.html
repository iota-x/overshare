Paste one Discord link. It checks it for you. That's the setup.

How it works

It watches what's in front of you, writes a sentence about it, and sends it to a Discord channel of your choosing.

👀

It notices

The app you're in, the file you're editing, the tab you're reading, the song playing behind it, and when you've wandered off.

✨

It writes

A warm one-liner, not a log entry. Free AI via Groq or a local model — or plain templates with no AI at all.

💌

She gets it

A rich card in Discord, with links and thumbnails. She can reply, react, and ask for a photo — and you're told every time.

A real settings window

Seven pages, and it checks its own work — the webhook is verified as you type, and each AI provider's models are listed from your own account. Nothing to edit by hand.

What it does

  • Per-site smarts — YouTube, Twitch, Reddit, GitHub and friends get their own emoji, colour and phrasing.
  • Now playing — “watching X while Y plays”, even when Spotify's in the background.
  • Peek on demand — she can ask for a webcam photo or a screenshot, and you're pinged every time.
  • Recaps — a nightly summary and a weekly Wrapped: top apps, what you watched, streaks, late nights.
  • Two-way — with a bot, her replies and reactions come back to you as notifications.
  • An off-switch — one click to pause. Because sometimes you want privacy.
Heads up on first launch. The app isn't signed by a paid developer account, so macOS will say it “could not verify” it — right-click the app and choose Open, once. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. Everything is open source if you'd rather read it first.
